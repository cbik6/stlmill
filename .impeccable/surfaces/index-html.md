# Hub (index.html) — surface brief

Scope: the STLmill landing page. Visitor mode: Persuade. Audience: home FDM makers arriving from maker communities (two states, equal: knows-what-a-clicker-is and curious newcomer). Job: understand STLmill in seconds and open a tool. Action: open the Clicker Generator. Proof: a live 3D clicker that rebuilds as the visitor types. Constraints: no invented numbers or social proof; future tools shown as open slots, never as available; licence is personal-use only; credit + quiet tip link; nothing leaves the browser.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The hub is a workshop pegboard: every generator hangs as a printed filament swatch card, every future tool is an unlit shadow-board outline on an empty hook. It refuses the headline-subline-button-then-card-grid arrangement.

OWN-WORLD: Tempered-hardboard brown ground (#A47A52) with a 48 px perforated hole grid; swatch cards in brand orange #F07A2A, filament teal #1FA39A and PLA white #F2EFE9 with embossed Barlow Semi Condensed labels; steel hooks; near-black #1C1B19 tab actions; fixed-position digit readouts for millimetres; wordmark in Space Grotesk 700 on a small pinned swatch.

STORY: A maker sees a real clicker being made as they type their own text, reads that it all runs in the browser, and opens the mill. A newcomer learns the lineup from what hangs and what is only outlined.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed pegboard. Brand swatch pinned top-left. The Clicker card (≈780×620 at 1440) hangs left: embossed CLICKER label, one-line sub, live rotating 3D clicker, in-place text field, two-digit size readout with mm, and the card's own black tab "Open the mill" as the primary action. Right: three dashed shadow outlines on hooks (Keychain, Coaster, Box). Bottom-right: a hanging tag with the in-browser promise, licence line, credit and tip link. Signature interaction: typing in the card rebuilds the 3D clicker; motion grammar: hover lifts a card off its hook with a deeper shadow (160 ms ease-out), size changes tick the digit readout, nothing else animates.

FORM: Swatch Wall, candidate 3 of 7 on the grounded list (assigned by the roll). seed:90163a2d7446d555

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
